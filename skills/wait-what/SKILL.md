# wait-what

The reader lost the thread. Re-pitch your last answer from one step back.

Different from `bro` (which just de-jargons the same answer): here you restore
the CONTEXT and the DIRECTION — what problem this addresses, where the
reasoning is heading, and why it matters — so the reader can catch up.

## Rules

- Start one step back: name the problem or goal this connects to in one
  sentence.
- Then re-pitch the point: what you are saying and where it leads.
- Add just enough context to make it land — no full re-derivation, no wall of
  text.
- Plain words. Explain any needed technical term inline.
- Keep exact paths, commands, numbers, and code unchanged.
- End with the concrete next step or takeaway, so the reader knows what to do
  with it.
